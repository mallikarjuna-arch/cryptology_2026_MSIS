| No. | Command | One-line explanation | Example |

| **i** | `whois manipal.edu` | Shows **domain registration and ownership information**. | `whois google.com` |
| **ii** | `dig manipal.edu` | Performs a **DNS lookup** and shows DNS records such as IP information. | `dig google.com` |
| **iii** | `nslookup manipal.edu` | Finds the **IP address of a domain using DNS**. | `nslookup google.com` |
| **iv** | `host manipal.edu` | Quickly shows the **IP address/DNS information** of a domain. | `host google.com` |
| **v** | `theHarvester -d manipal.edu -b yahoo -l 100` | Collects **publicly available information** such as emails and hosts from Yahoo, limited to 100 results. | `theHarvester -d example.com -b yahoo -l 100` |
| **vi** | `theHarvester -d manipal.edu -b crtsh,hackertarget,rapiddns,urlscan` | Collects publicly available **domains, hosts and related information** from multiple sources. | `theHarvester -d example.com -b crtsh,hackertarget,rapiddns,urlscan` |
| **vii** | `site:manipal.edu filetype:pdf` | Google search to find **PDF files available on manipal.edu**. | `site:google.com filetype:pdf` |
| **viii** | `site:manipal.edu inurl:login` | Searches for pages on the domain containing **“login” in the URL**. | `site:example.com inurl:login` |
| **ix** | `site:manipal.edu intitle:password` | Searches for pages on the domain having **“password” in the page title**. | `site:example.com intitle:password` |
| **x** | `sudo arp-scan --localnet` | Scans the **local network** to find connected hosts/devices. | `sudo arp-scan --localnet` |
